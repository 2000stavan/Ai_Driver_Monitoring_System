## What is this study?
We are building and testing computer programs that detect driver tiredness and distraction (for example, using a phone or drinking while driving) and harsh driving. Most existing systems were built with data from other countries. We are recording a small amount of data with Indian drivers in Indian cars to test how well these programs work here.

## What will I be asked to do?
Depending on the session you agree to:

- **Session A (parked car, about 25 minutes):** sit in the driver's seat of a **parked car with the engine off** and act out everyday actions on cue — eating a snack, drinking water, holding a phone to your ear, typing on a phone, checking mirrors, touching your face — while a phone camera records your face and upper body.
- **Session B (empty ground, about 20 minutes, licensed drivers only):** drive slowly (maximum 30 km/h) in an **empty, closed space** and perform braking, acceleration and turning manoeuvres while phones record video and motion-sensor data.
- **Session C (optional, normal drive):** drive normally and legally on regular roads while phones record. **You will never be asked to do anything distracting while driving on a road.**

## What is recorded?
- Video of your face and upper body (and, in Sessions B/C, the road ahead).
- Phone motion-sensor and GPS data (Sessions B/C).
- Basic information: age range, gender, driving experience, whether you wore glasses / sunglasses / a head covering. **No other personal details.**

## How will my data be used and protected?
- Used **only for academic research** in this project (training and testing the programs, and reporting results).
- Stored on the researcher's private computer and private cloud storage; files are named with a code (e.g. P07), **not your name**. Signed consent forms are kept separately from the data.
- **Your face will not be shown publicly** (in papers, presentations, demo videos or any public dataset) **unless you give separate permission below.**
- Data will not be sold or used commercially.
- The data may be shared with the project's academic supervisor(s) for research only.

## Risks and safety
- Session A takes place in a parked car with the engine off.
- Session B takes place only in an empty, closed area at low speed, with seatbelts on and a second person present. You may stop at any time.
- There are no other expected risks.

## Your rights
- Taking part is **voluntary**.
- You can **stop at any time** during recording, without giving a reason.
- You can **ask for your data to be deleted** at any time by contacting the researcher, quoting your participant code.
